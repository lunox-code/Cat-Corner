A FEW GOOD HABITS

Little things
cats need.

A happy cat needs more than cuddles. Start with a safe home, a little patience, and the basics below.

  • 01
    Fresh water

    Keep a clean bowl available and refresh it each day.

  • 02
    A quiet safe spot

    Give your cat a cozy place where they can rest undisturbed.

  • 03
    Play and attention

    Short play sessions and gentle company help cats stay engaged.

For health questions, check with a veterinarian who knows your cat.
